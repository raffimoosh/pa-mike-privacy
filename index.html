Privacy Policy

Public Adjuster Mike · Effective August 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Public Adjuster Mike ("we," "us") collects when you respond to our advertising, and how we use it. We keep it simple and we do not sell your information.

What we collect

When you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, we receive the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The ZIP code of your property
  • Details you share about your property damage or insurance claim

How we use it

We use your information to contact you about your free claim review and, if you choose to work with us, to provide public adjusting services. By submitting a form, you agree that we may call, text, or email you at the contact details you provided to discuss your claim. You can opt out any time: reply STOP to end texts, or tell us by phone or message and we will stop contacting you.

What we share

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (for example phone, scheduling, or customer management systems), and only as needed, or when the law requires it.

Forms delivered by Meta

Our lead forms are hosted and delivered by Meta (Facebook and Instagram). Meta's handling of your data, including form prefill, is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your request, provide our services, and meet legal and record keeping requirements, then we delete it.

Your choices and California rights

California residents may ask us what personal information we hold about them, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Make any of these requests through the contact options below and we will honor them.

Contact

Reach us by message through our Public Adjuster Mike Facebook Page or by the phone number shown in our advertising.

Changes

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
